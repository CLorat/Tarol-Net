# Contributing

Tarol is licensed under the GNU AGPL-3.0-only and is also offered under a commercial licence by the copyright holder
(see `NOTICE`). So that both stay possible, **a contribution can be merged only after its author has signed a contributor
licence agreement (CLA) with the copyright holder**. Open an issue first and ask for the CLA; pull requests without one are
not merged.

How to build, test and propose a change: `AGENTS.md` and `docs/migration/plan.md#pull-requests`.
